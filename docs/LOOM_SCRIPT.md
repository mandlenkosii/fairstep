## Part 1: Pitch (0:00–2:00), landing page on screen

**[0:00] Hook**
> Last year the CCMA registered over 193,000 cases, and more than half were unfair dismissal disputes. A big share of them come from small businesses, and many employers lose not because they were wrong, but because the **process** wasn't fair, or the paperwork was wrong, lost or never written down.

**[0:20] Customer**
> Picture Thabo. He runs Kasi Kitchen in Soweto with eight staff. He has no HR department, and he can't pay a lawyer every time someone's late or the till is short. When he gets it wrong, the law says he can owe up to twelve months' wages in compensation.

**[0:40] Solution**
> FairStep is a guided disciplinary process for small businesses. It walks the owner from incident to investigation, notice, hearing, outcome and appeal, following the 2025 Code of Good Practice: Dismissal. It **blocks unfair steps** and says why in plain language, generates the letters, lets the owner's labour consultant review and sign off, and exports the whole case as one CCMA-ready bundle. Every step fair, every step on record.

**[1:05] Impact**
> Our north-star metric is the percentage of cases where every step is completed and documented, because procedural gaps are what lose cases. We also track time to prepare a hearing, and later, cases lost and compensation paid.

**[1:20] Money**
> Owners pay R149 a month for up to 10 employees, or R299 for up to 50. Consultants pay R799 to manage up to 15 clients. One consultant-run hearing costs R3,000 to R8,000, so preventing a single lost case pays for years of FairStep.

**[1:35] Go-to-market and competition**
> Our channel is labour consultants: ten partners who each bring 10 to 30 clients, because FairStep gives them complete files and less admin. Then business chambers and owner WhatsApp groups. The free CCMA app and template sites give documents; HR suites are too heavy for eight people. FairStep is the only one that **enforces** the process and keeps an audit trail, priced for a small business. And it's process guidance, not legal advice.

## Part 2: Live demo (2:00–5:00)

**[2:00] Owner dashboard (window A, go to Dashboard)**
> This is Thabo's dashboard: open cases, hearings this week, and the share of cases fully documented. The "Needs your attention" list tells him what to do next.

**[2:15] A blocked step (open Zanele Nkosi's case)**
> Zanele missed two shifts without phoning in. The case is at Investigation, and FairStep won't let Thabo issue a notice yet. It says: *record what you found first, so the allegations are clear.*

Fill in **Who investigated:** Thabo Mokoena · **Who raised the complaint:** Sipho Ndlovu · **Findings:** "Shift register shows Zanele absent on 17 and 18 November; no call or message received." → **Save details** → **Continue**.

**[2:45] The rules in action (notice step)**
> Now the notice. Let me try to cut a corner.

**Allegations:** "Absence without leave on 17 and 18 November 2026." · **Notice given:** today · **Hearing:** *tomorrow* · **Venue:** Back office · **Chairperson:** *Sipho Ndlovu* → **Save details**.
> FairStep stops me twice: the employee must get at least two working days to prepare, and it even gives me the earliest fair date. And the complainant can't chair the hearing.

Change **Hearing** to a date at least 3 working days away and **Chairperson** to "Naledi Dlamini" → **Save details**.
> Fixed, and now I can continue.

**[3:25] Letter (Letters and documents → Notice of hearing → Generate PDF)**
> One click generates the notice of hearing as a PDF: allegations, the employee's rights, the date and venue, and a not-legal-advice footer. It's stored privately and logged on the timeline.

Open it briefly.

**[3:45] Send for review**
> Before he acts, Thabo sends the case to his consultant.

Note: "Please check the notice before I give it to Zanele." → **Send for review**.

**[3:55] Consultant (window B, refresh the review queue)**
> This is Naledi, the labour consultant. She only sees the businesses she advises; Bakwena Build's cases simply don't exist for her. Zanele's case is waiting for review. She can read everything but can't change the facts.

Open the case → Comment: "Allegations are specific and the notice period is fair. Approved." → **Approve**.

**[4:25] Back to the owner (window A, refresh)**
> Thabo sees "Approved by consultant", and the timeline shows every action: who did it, their role, and when. Nobody can edit or delete these entries, not even an admin.

**[4:40] CCMA bundle (Export case bundle)**
> If Zanele ever refers this to the CCMA, one click exports the whole case: a cover page, every step with its date, the full record, the timeline and the documents.

**[4:50] Close**
> Under the hood, every table has row-level security, the rules run on the server, and CI tests it all on every change. FairStep: every step fair, every step on record. Thanks for watching.
